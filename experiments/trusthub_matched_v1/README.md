# TrustHub comparability preflight

## Admission decision

Keep the nine-host v2 cohort and the new RS232 triplet in development. The nine-host pilot passes its historical scalar-size screen. That screen uses a 0.90 point-estimate cutoff in either direction. Inclusion of 0.5 in a wide confidence interval does not establish practical equivalence.

The size validation independently reconstructs directed cell-to-cell edges from net drivers and sinks, checks all 27 counts against the saved audit, and computes the exact empirical distribution of resampling nine whole hosts with replacement. Each host carries both negative comparisons. The pointwise 95% percentile intervals describe variation among inspected hosts.

The original fanout probe ranks 0.556 against original and 0.500 against benign. All 27 output-XOR and cone-equivalence vectors are zero. Those rules had no coverage. A neutral-name fraction ranks every v2 design above every same-label TrustHub reference in the four-family typed development cohort. Cell count alone gives origin AUROC 0.889 on clean and 0.861 on Trojan arms, weighting families equally. Those effects include differences between host populations.

## Bounded construction test

Use a copy of the inspected RS232-T2100 clean receiver. Preserve its interface, original assignment site, naming and source format. Apply the same conditional edit in both synthetic arms and retain the genuine original as a third comparison. Each synthetic arm is explicitly labeled in its recipe. The original archive and the reserved hosts are excluded from writes.

At the receive-data assignment, the benign conditional maps A6 to A6. The synthetic fault maps A6 to A7. The runner sends all 256 possible byte values as serial frames after reset and checks the received byte and ready signal at the end of every frame, on source and mapped Verilog. It records source hashes and complete gate-type histograms for all three arms.

The benign and synthetic histograms are identical, each with 208 cells. The original has 190. The 208-cell construction falls inside the 198?229-cell range of the four inspected native RS232 Trojan examples. This is overlap on one measurement on one shared host. It provides no independent-host replication and no detector score. Training and broad generation remain on hold.

## Next gate

Freeze host-family splits and practical equivalence margins before building additional variants. Apply one class-independent frontend, normalization, and synthesis policy to native and synthetic examples. Keep raw and normalized results and origin labels. Evaluate source identity separately within each class, first on shared development hosts and then on held-out host families. Compare gate-type deltas, identifier/comment statistics, format and tokenizer windows, port fanout and output-cone features. Record motif coverage. Keep both original and benign controls, preserve all generated siblings in the same host fold, and verify every candidate against a functional oracle. A detector claim still requires a separate locked transfer experiment.
