2.Trojan
  Trojan Description
	Trojan trigger is a combinational comparator. Trigger input probability is 1.057e-7 while it is externally controlled. Whenever Trojan gets triggered, its payload gains control over xmit_doneH primary output signal. 

  Trojan Taxonomy
	Insertion phase: Design
	Abstraction level: Layout-level
	Activation mechanism: Externally conditionally triggered
	Effects: Change functionality
	Physical characteristics: Functional
