2.Trojan
  Trojan Description
	Trojan is a chain of inverters which is driven by a the design main clock. 

  Trojan Taxonomy
	Insertion phase: Design
	Abstraction level: Layout-level
	Activation mechanism: Externally conditionally triggered
	Effects: Reduce design reliability
	Physical characteristics: Functional 

  Trojan Circuit
    AND2X1  U300 (.A(ena), .B(sys_clk), .Y(sys_clk_l));
    INVX1  U301 (.A(sys_clk_l), .Y(sys_clk_h));
    INVX1  U302 (.A(sys_clk_h), .Y(sys_clk_hl));
    INVX1  U303 (.A(sys_clk_hl), .Y(sys_clk_hh));
