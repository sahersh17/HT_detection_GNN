2.Trojan
  Trojan Description
	Trojan trigger is a sequential comparator. Trigger input probability is 8.45e-7 while it is externally controlled. Whenever Trojan gets triggered, its payload gains control over xmit_doneH and rec_readyH primary output signals. 


  Trojan Taxonomy
	Insertion phase: Design
	Abstraction level: Layout-level
	Activation mechanism: Externally conditionally triggered
	Effects: Change functionality
	Physical characteristics: Functional
