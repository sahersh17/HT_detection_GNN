2.Trojan
  Trojan Description
	Trojan trigger is a sequential comparator. Trigger input probability is 3.37e-6  while it is externally controlled. Whenever Trojan gets triggered, its payload gains control over xmit_doneH primary output signal.

  Trojan Taxonomy
	Abstraction level: Layout-level
	Activation mechanism: Externally conditionally triggered
	Effects: Change functionality
	Physical characteristics: Functional
